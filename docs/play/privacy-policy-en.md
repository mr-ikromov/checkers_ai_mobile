## Privacy Policy (English)

**Last updated:** 8 November 2026

**Shashka AI** does not collect, store or transmit any
personal data.

## What data is collected

None. Specifically:

- the app has no sign-up and no accounts;
- the app has no server — it never connects anywhere;
- it does not request the Android internet permission at all;
- no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs are used;
- no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## What is stored on your device

The following is kept only inside your phone, in the app's private
storage, and is never sent anywhere:

- the selected language;
- the selected side (white or black);
- theme (light/dark), animation, haptics and coordinate preferences;
- your win / draw / loss counts.

The neural-network model stays encrypted inside the app itself. It is never written to device storage and is decrypted only in memory while you play.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear it at any
time from the app's settings ("Reset record") or with "Clear data" in
the Android settings.

## Backups

If Android's automatic backup is enabled on your device, the
preferences above may be copied to your own Google Drive. That is a
Google platform feature; the app has no access to that copy and
cannot read it. You can turn it off in the Android settings.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page
