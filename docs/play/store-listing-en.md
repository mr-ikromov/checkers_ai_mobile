## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Russian checkers against a real neural network. No internet, no sign-up, no ads.

◆ WHO YOU ARE PLAYING

Your opponent is a transformer neural network trained the AlphaZero way. It learned checkers entirely by playing against itself — no human games, no opening book, no hand-written evaluation. Every idea it uses, from sacrifices to long capture chains to king endgames, it found on its own.

The network runs on your phone. It reads the position and answers instantly, in well under a second.

◆ SEE WHAT HAPPENS ON EVERY MOVE

• EVALUATION — how the position scores from your side (−1 to +1)
• CONFIDENCE — how strongly the network preferred the move it played

The readouts move with the game, so you can see exactly where you went wrong and where you took over.

◆ FULL RUSSIAN CHECKERS RULES

• Capturing is compulsory; men capture in all four diagonal directions, forward and backward
• Flying kings move and capture at any distance
• Capture chains with the landing-square restriction
• The "Turkish strike": captured pieces stay as blockers until the chain ends
• Promotion in the middle of a chain, which then continues as a king
• Draw by threefold repetition and by the 30-move rule

The rules engine is checked move by move against an open-source reference implementation across thousands of games — no differences.

◆ IN THE APP

• 3 languages: Uzbek, Russian, English
• Play as White or Black
• Local record: wins / draws / losses
• Built-in rules reference
• Light and dark theme
• Haptic feedback can be turned off

◆ PRIVACY

The app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads. It does not even request the internet permission — it does not have one.

Everything, the neural network included, runs on your device.
```
